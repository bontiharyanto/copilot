# SOP-IT-001: VPN Access

**Category:** Network access  
**Owner:** IT Service Desk  
**Review cadence:** Every 12 months

## Purpose

Provide a standard procedure for employees who cannot connect to the corporate VPN.

## Procedure

1. Confirm that the device has an active internet connection.
2. Confirm that the employee is using the approved VPN client.
3. Ask the employee to sign in with their corporate account and complete MFA.
4. If the client reports an expired session, sign out, restart the client, and sign in again.
5. If the client reports a certificate or profile error, reinstall the approved corporate VPN
   profile. Do not install profiles from unapproved sources.
6. Confirm access by opening an internal application or SharePoint site.

## Escalation

Create a support ticket when the issue continues after these steps. Include the employee's
username, device name, VPN error message, time of failure, and whether other users are affected.

## Security note

Never request or record a user's password, MFA code, or recovery code.
